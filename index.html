The origin story

Karthick is a mechanical engineer who found his gamma moment in combustion research — spending his M.E. thesis measuring how a single-cylinder SI engine performs and emits when fuelled on Hythane20. Before that, a B.E. in Mechanical Engineering at PSG College of Technology and a state-topping Diploma gave him the fundamentals: heat transfer, thermodynamics, and a habit of chasing the "why" behind every reading.

His path since graduation hasn't stayed in one lab — shift leadership on a factory floor, teaching mechanical engineering as an Assistant Professor, and caregiving in the UK have each sharpened a different kind of strength: technical precision, the patience to explain it, and the resilience to show up for people.
